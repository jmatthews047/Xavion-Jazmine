The Proposal

The perfect ending to an imperfect plan.

On June 19, 2026, Zay planned for them to spend a beautiful day together—starting with a trip to the park, followed by dinner and a night at a hotel. But, of course, the weather had other plans.

They spent the first part of the day cozied up at home watching movies while waiting for the rain to pass. What Jazmine didn’t know was that Zay was patiently waiting for her mother to return home so he could get the ring from her.

Once they arrived at the hotel, they began getting ready for dinner. Then Zay told Jazmine there was one more thing they needed to do before leaving.

Jazmine turned around and found him on one knee, asking her to marry him. The day may not have gone according to plan, but it ended in the most perfect way possible—with a “yes” and the beginning of their forever.
